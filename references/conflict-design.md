# Conflict design

Conflict is a constrained choice. The strongest long arcs connect an immediate
obstacle to a deeper disagreement without making every scene louder.

## Useful axes

- person against person: incompatible goals or trust;
- person against group: rules, status, or belonging;
- person against environment: scarcity, distance, time, or physical danger;
- person against self: fear, desire, guilt, or identity;
- person against consequence: an earlier choice narrowing the future.

## Escalation test

For each beat, record what becomes harder, what new information appears, and what
resource is spent. Escalation may be emotional, social, informational, spatial,
or physical. If only the scale changes while the choice stays the same, add a
new cost or change the relationship.

## Long-form root

Give an arc a durable question or contradiction. Let chapters expose different
facets of it, and settle only the portion earned by the current evidence. A root
is a planning aid, not permission to withhold arbitrary facts or force a twist.
