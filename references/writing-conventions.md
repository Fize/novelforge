# 写作约定

用户可见的流程说明和创作建议使用简体中文；机器字段、状态值和命令保持稳定。

These conventions are format-level defaults only. A project's language,
measurement units, chapter length, paragraphing, and register are explicit
configuration, not hidden genre assumptions.

## Long-form defaults

- Every chapter declares its purpose, dependencies, change, and next question.
- Chapter length is a project setting, not a universal number.
- Scenes may slow down or remain quiet when that serves character, tension, or
  information.
- Repeated beats must record what changes; otherwise they are flagged as an
  advisory warning.

## Consistency

- Names, terms, time, measurements, and point of view must match the project
  profile.
- A character's action and speech must be consistent with the current state,
  knowledge, goals, and relationships.
- The script checks structured consistency; literary preference remains a
  configurable diagnostic.
