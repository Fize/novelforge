# Milestone review

Milestones are optional planning checkpoints for long projects. They summarize
accepted facts rather than replacing chapter Settlements.

## Required content

- covered chapter IDs and their settlement hashes;
- durable entity, relationship, knowledge, time, and location changes;
- hook lifecycle changes and unresolved questions;
- the next arc or checkpoint objective;
- risks that need a new dependency or blueprint field.

## Gate

At a project-defined interval, write a Markdown or JSON note under the project's
planning directory. Never edit chapter state by hand. A milestone that conflicts
with a Settlement is a review finding and must be corrected at the source.
