# Conflict and action scene contract

This is a genre-neutral scene contract. A project may define combat, debate,
chase, investigation, or other conflict-specific rules in a pack.

## Required scene facts

- Participants and their current capabilities are declared.
- The immediate objective and opposing objective are explicit.
- Each exchange changes position, information, resources, or relationship.
- The outcome follows the declared constraints or records the cost of breaking
  one.
- The scene leaves a changed state for the next chapter.

## Advisory craft checks

- Vary action, observation, decision, and consequence instead of listing moves.
- Use setting and sensory detail when they affect choice or comprehension.
- Let strategy emerge from character knowledge and values.
- Do not require a fixed number of senses, tactics, paragraphs, or power levels.

The blueprint may activate an engine card for this scene. The script validates
the card's applicability and evidence; it does not impose an unselected genre.
